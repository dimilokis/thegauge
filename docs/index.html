How to read it

Four reads.
One five-second glance.

Here's exactly what every column on the dashboard means. No jargon left unexplained — each one answers a question you already have.

I
The Gauge Score
Am I early — or am I late?

One number, 0 to 100. It rolls up three things you'd otherwise check by hand — where the price sits in its 90-day range, its momentum, and how far it's stretched from trend. Near 0, it's cheap by its own history. Near 100, it's stretched thin. It's not a prediction — it's a fast read on where a coin stands relative to itself.

18
II
The Move
Is this real, or just a Tuesday?

We take today's volume and volatility and compare them to how that coin usually behaves, in standard deviations. Normal is a quiet day. Extreme means something's actually going on. So a candle that looks terrifying but is routine for that coin doesn't trick you into selling.

Extreme · 3.1σ
III
The Driver
Is it the coin — or just Bitcoin?

A coin jumps 9% and everyone assumes the project is mooning. Usually it's just Bitcoin towing the market. We split the move into the part BTC explains and the part that's the coin itself. Independent means something real is happening here specifically. Bitcoin-driven means it's just the tide coming in. This is the column I've genuinely never seen anywhere else.

Independentonly 22% from BTC
IV
The Verdict
In one word, where does it stand?

The plain-English summary — Oversold, Neutral, Overbought, or Strong Uptrend. It's calibrated by liquidity, not one-size-fits-all: our own multi-year backtest found that for the most liquid coins, "overbought" rarely reverses — it's usually just strong momentum, so we call it what it is instead of promising a reversal that doesn't show up in the data.

Under the hood

Five calm columns.
A lot of plumbing.

What you see is simple on purpose. Getting there means cleaning, normalising and decomposing years of messy market history for 100 coins — the boring, unglamorous work that makes the simple part trustworthy.

01

No Ghost Data

When a coin gets delisted, the data feeds keep spitting out fake candles for it. Those zombies quietly wreck any stat built on top of them. We find where each coin actually stopped trading and cut everything after. You only ever read real history.

02

Z-Score Baselines

The Move column isn't a vibe. It's a hard standard-deviation measure: how far today sits from how that specific coin has behaved over the last 90 days. A 6% drop can be a 0.5σ shrug for one coin and a 3σ event for another.

03

BTC Decomposition

Every move gets pulled apart: how much was Bitcoin, how much was the coin on its own. That separation is the whole difference between a price change and something you can actually think about.

Runs on Binance market data — cleaned, normalised and re-scored across the 100 most liquid coins by 24h volume.
