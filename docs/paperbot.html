Research Lines — Paper Trading

The receipts.

Two strategies running live in simulation, public data, no real money, nothing edited after the fact. Every position, every close, shown as it happened.

Strategy 1

Dip-buy, ML-filtered

Entry on a sharp 5-minute-bar drop (top-200 by volume) confirmed by a 2-stage ML classifier; exit at a fixed 1h hold or -5% stop, whichever comes first.

Not related to the Gauge Score above — different data (5-minute bars, not daily), different method (ML classifier, not percentile/RSI/Driver). Shown here for transparency, not sold as a signal.

Strategy 2

Gauge oversold, BTC-driven

Entry when a Tier A/B asset hits Gauge Score <5 (extreme oversold) and the drop is >60% explained by BTC (the Driver metric) — a coin falling because the whole market fell, not for its own reason. Exit is price-only: -5% stop or +10% target, no fixed hold.

This one does use the Gauge Score (and Tier, and Driver) as its entry filter — it's the one backtested edge that survived lookahead/survivorship/robustness checks on 2020-2026 history: N=346, winrate 50.0%, EV +2.49%/trade, MaxDD -22.6%. Modest, real, not a guarantee.
